## Hard gates that points cannot compensate for

Even at90/100, do not submit as ready if:

- The human-rights/financial-freedom connection is invented or unsupported, or the intended fund's
  eligibility is misrepresented.
- Content/data permissions or participant consent are missing for the work actually performed.
- There is an unresolved critical authorization, secret-handling or private-data egress failure
  in the advertised pilot scope, or users are misled about who can access their data.
- A claimed capability cannot be inspected/reproduced, or the publication package exposes
  credentials, private feedback or participant information.
- The applicant's funding history/budget or required reference information is missing or inaccurate.
- The report calls a planned pilot a completed case study, an approved-card response an LLM output,
  ordinary rental a TEE, or the internal score an HRF endorsement.

At the review, document which remaining noncritical criteria, if any, are funded future milestones.
The internal90% threshold may be stricter than the funder's application prerequisites. It must not
be interpreted as permission to start a human pilot before its relevant consent/security gates.

Four-of-seven administration, confidential inference, new language training and private member
messaging are not silently added to or credited in this education scope. If promised in the
application, add their specific acceptance evidence and re-agree the rubric before measurement;
do not drop a difficult criterion after results arrive to inflate readiness.
