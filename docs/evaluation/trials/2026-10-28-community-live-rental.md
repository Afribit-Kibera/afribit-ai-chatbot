## What this proves and what comes next

This demonstrates the bounded functional proposition on synthetic data: local community retrieval and
account context can feed only selected authorized material to a real replaceable rented model and
store results through the normal API, without training or owning the GPU. Offline negative tests
cover disallowed egress, outage/keys/quota and endpoint replacement; those are not secretly real
GPU-load experiments. Ordinary rental still trusts its host; no TEE/full-private/cheapest claim.

Next: native blind review and improvements against its findings; export per-request measurements in
